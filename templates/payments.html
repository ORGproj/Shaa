ACCOUNTING WORKFLOW

Core Responsibilities

Record-Keeping & Tracking

Record dated transactions, search receipts, and review accounts receivable information. Bank reconciliation and payable workflows are future extensions.

Financial Reporting

Payment history can be filtered by day or date range. Balance sheets, income statements, cash-flow reports, budgets and billing statements are on the Financial Reports page.

Budgeting & Forecasting

Payment history can support future budget reviews. Budget plans and forecasting models are not currently automated.

Auditing & Risk Control

Reference numbers and activity logs support traceability. Review records regularly and restrict access to authorized staff.
